Key Insights

    

    

      

        
📈

        
Near-linear aggregate scaling

        
Cumulative generation throughput rises from 21 tok/s (1 session) to 108 tok/s (7 sessions) — roughly 5.1× throughput gain. Each added session contributes meaningful aggregate capacity.

      

      

        
⚡

        
Sweet spot at 4 concurrent

        
Four parallel sessions deliver the best per-run generation rate under load (~18.5 tok/s) with strong aggregate throughput (72.7 tok/s) in just 21.7 s. Best balance of speed and total output.

      

      

        
⚠️

        
Per-session degradation

        
Individual session throughput drops ~26% under load (21 → ~15.5 tok/s at 7 concurrent). Resource contention increases elapsed time per request from ~21 s to ~28 s.
